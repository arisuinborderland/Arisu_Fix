QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Important things to know before you contact Arisu.

Is account recovery guaranteed?

No. Every case is different, and the platform makes the final decision. Assistance cannot guarantee that an account will be restored.

How much is the service fee?

Any applicable service fee should be explained directly and agreed before work begins. This website does not promise a fixed price for every case.

How long will recovery take?

There is no universal timeline. It depends on the account issue and the platform's review process. Be cautious of guaranteed completion times.

What information should I prepare?

Start with a short description of the issue and the platform involved. Never send passwords, login codes, 2FA codes, backup codes, or other secrets.

Can every disabled or hacked account be recovered?

No. Availability of official recovery options and the final outcome depend on the platform and the specific case.

Is this an official Meta service?

No. Arisu Recovery Contact is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta, Facebook, or Instagram.
