The review-slot image files in this folder are display assets referenced by the
site's review sections. To replace a slot, replace the corresponding image while
keeping its filename, or update the image path in index.html and reviews.html.

Before publishing a screenshot as a customer testimonial, verify that it is
actually about Arisu Fix and that the customer has granted permission. Images
that mention another service must be clearly identified as references and must
not be presented as testimonials for Arisu Fix. Redact private or identifying
information before publishing.
