ARISU RECOVERY CONTACT — CUSTOMER REVIEW SCREENSHOTS

The homepage and Customer Reviews page currently use three review slots only.
- Slot 01 is a reference image that mentions Pinoy Cyber Tech; it is not an Arisu testimonial.
- Slots 02 and 03 are clearly marked placeholders until genuine customer screenshots are available.

HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN SCREENSHOTS
1. Put a genuine customer screenshot in this folder, for example customer-review-2.jpg.
2. Open index.html and reviews.html in a text editor.
3. Find the relevant review-slot image source and replace it with your new filename.
4. For a genuine screenshot, remove the is-placeholder class and disabled attribute from that card's image button so it can be opened full-size.
5. Update the image alt text and any reference/placeholder caption.

IMAGE FITTING
The homepage previews preserve a portrait screenshot's aspect ratio and use object-fit: contain. Avoid forcing a tall fixed image height or stretching the screenshot.

PRIVACY / TRUST
- Do not present another business's testimonial as feedback for Arisu.
- Get permission before showing names, profile photos, private messages, or account details.
- Blur sensitive details and never expose passwords, login codes, recovery codes, email addresses, or phone numbers.
- Do not invent ratings or customer experiences. Results vary and are not guaranteed.
